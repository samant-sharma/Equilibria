Privacy Policy Equilibria -The Modern Growth Capital Platform

Effective Date: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

At Equilibria, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your data when you use our website, platform, or services (collectively, the “Platform”).

By using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you should not use the Platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Personal Information you provide:

  • Account and profile information (name, email, phone number, password)
  • Identity verification data required for KYC/AML (government-issued ID, date of birth, address, source of funds information)
  • Company information (for issuers raising capital): business details, ownership structure, financial information
  • Investment-related data (portfolio, investment history, preferences, watchlist)
  • Communication data (support requests, messages, survey responses)

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and usage information (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on the Platform)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

Information from third parties:

  • KYC/AML verification partners
  • Licensed intermediaries and custodians
  • Payment processors

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Perform required KYC/AML verification and comply with regulatory obligations
  • Facilitate capital raises and investment opportunities
  • Process transactions and manage investments
  • Provide customer support and platform communications
  • Improve and personalize your experience
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements in the United States, GCC, and European Union

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only when necessary and with appropriate safeguards:

  • With licensed intermediaries, broker-dealers, and custodians to facilitate regulated offerings
  • With regulated KYC/AML verification providers
  • With regulators or authorities when required by law
  • With service providers who assist in operating the Platform (under strict confidentiality agreements)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to operate the Platform and analytical cookies to understand usage and improve services. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption, access controls and regular audits. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct or delete your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@equilibria.net We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

8. International Data Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred and processed outside your country of residence, including in the United States and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

Equilibria serves users and investors across the Middle East & North Africa (MENA) and Asia regions. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for all cross-border data transfers in compliance with applicable laws

9. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on the Platform and updating the effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: Email: privacy@equlibria.net

By using Equilibria, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agreed to this Privacy Policy.
