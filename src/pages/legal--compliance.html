Compliance

Updated 10/22/2026

Our Commitment to Compliance

At Equilibria, compliance is the foundation of everything we do. As a provider of ownership management software, we operate with the highest standards of transparency, shareholder protection, and regulatory adherence so both companies raising capital and their shareholders can participate with complete confidence.

Regulatory Responsibility

Equilibria provides software only. Issuers and their counsel are responsible for compliance with the securities laws of each jurisdiction in which they offer.

KYC / AML Verification

Every purchaser completes a secure Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification process before participating in any offering. We partner with globally regulated verification providers to ensure:

  • Identity confirmation
  • Source of funds validation
  • Jurisdictional eligibility checks

This process protects all parties and maintains the integrity of the platform.

Funds & Custody

Equilibria does not receive, hold, transmit or custody investor funds, digital assets or securities. Any payment, escrow or custody is handled directly between the issuer and its regulated providers.

Disclosures & Risk Notice

Buying or holding securities of private and growth-stage companies involves risk, including the potential loss of principal and limited liquidity. Past performance is not indicative of future results. All offerings include detailed risk disclosures in their official documents. We strongly recommend reviewing these materials and consulting your own financial, legal and tax advisors before buying any securities.

Equilibria does not provide investment, legal, or tax advice. Nothing on this platform constitutes a solicitation or offer to sell securities in any jurisdiction where such an offer would be unlawful.
