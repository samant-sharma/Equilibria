Post-Close Operations

Closing is the midpoint of a private-market transaction. Equilibria supports the ongoing operating layer that follows.

Schedule a Consultation
Operations

Keep the transaction organized after close

Post-close operations require consistent records, communications, reporting, and compliance workflows.

01

Cap table management

Maintain ownership records, transfer restrictions, and holder-level data for ongoing administration.

02

Compliance reporting

Track filing calendars, document status, eligibility updates, and jurisdiction-specific operating requirements.

03

Shareholder communications

Coordinate updates, notices, documents, and shareholder workflow status in a consistent operating environment.

04

Distribution records

Record distributions paid by the issuer or its paying agent. Equilibria does not make payments.

Continuity

A lifecycle beyond the closing table

Many middle-market transactions lose discipline after closing because the operating layer is fragmented across spreadsheets, inboxes, counsel updates, and transfer records.

Equilibria keeps the post-close workflow connected to the original transaction structure, giving issuers and advisors a clearer way to administer ownership, reporting, and communications over time.
