Company Description

Syndication Gamma LLC holds 5,881,666 Class B shares of GolfSuites Inc., a hospitality operator running the GolfSuites model across multiple markets.

Shares were transferred from Colonial Stock Transfer (book entries 900968–900973, account 1076-99999965, May 2026). Beneficial owner: Satori Partners, Inc. (Steven Taracevicz). Affiliation disclosure: Steven Taracevicz is the Founder & CEO of Equilibria NET Inc., so Equilibria’s founder has a financial interest in these shares.

An independent fair market value opinion by Stanton Park Consulting dated June 2025 assessed 4,285,833 of the 5,881,666 shares at $13.7576 per share (~$58.96M independently valued); the total position at that price equals approximately $80.9M.

The offering is structured under Regulation D 506(c). Offering terms, subscription agreement, and restricted-legend language are pending counsel review.

Mission Statement

To provide accredited investors with direct, digitized access to fractional equity in GolfSuites Inc. — a hospitality company operating under the GolfSuites concept. Working ownership records are kept on Equilibria’s software; the issuer or its transfer agent keeps the official share register.
